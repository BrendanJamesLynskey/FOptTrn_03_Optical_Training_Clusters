# Fourier Optics for Training 03 — Optical Engines in a Training Cluster

Where an optical transform engine would sit in a training cluster and whether it would shorten a step: the anatomy of a training step (forward, backward, gradient all-reduce, optimizer), data, tensor, pipeline and sequence parallelism, weight masks rewritten every optimizer step against the digital step time, an interactive step-time against mask-time explorer, layer-major scheduling against GPipe and 1F1B pipelines and activation memory, gradient accumulation as the compromise, why data-by-data products stay digital, the static Amdahl bound at cluster scale, DeMo's DCT of the momentum, error feedback, and payload against wire bytes (all-gather against all-reduce), the measured cost of a fixed frequency band in place of top-k and its captured energy, where the band could be computed in transit (GPU all-reduce, pluggable link module, in-network summation) and the break-even WAN bandwidths, time and energy to a target loss when training across data centres (with labelled DiLoCo-style and Llama-3-8B extrapolations), and an honest list of what does not map. The fixed-band measurements come from the Compute in Transit results site (https://compute-in-transit-results.vercel.app), on a 15.8M-parameter GPT.

Topics: Parallelism, Mask rewrites, Layer-major, Pipelines, Amdahl, DeMo, Fixed band, Cross-DC.
